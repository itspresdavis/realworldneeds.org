# Brand-Preserving Visual Audit

The established RWN identity is intact and should remain the source of truth: the bright RWN logo, green accent, light mobile finder surface, and detailed dark globe serve distinct roles. The desktop globe remains a high-value visual and interaction surface, while the mobile flow properly leads with the finder before the globe.

The approved additive focus is therefore limited to preserving these strengths while improving reliability around them: keep the direct route to matching services visibly available, maintain mobile-friendly core-need controls, and ensure the directory/service handoff remains source-forward. No logo, color-system, typography, globe-rendering, or globe-gesture replacement is in scope.
