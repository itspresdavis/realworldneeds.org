# RWN Advertising and Sponsorship Policy

## Core Principle

> **RWN never monetizes a person’s moment of need.**

Advertising or sponsorship may support the platform, but it must never appear inside, interrupt, influence, or resemble the process of finding or accessing help.

## Permanently Ad-Free Help Surfaces

The following must remain free of commercial advertising, sponsored ranking, affiliate placement, and promotional interruption:

- Individual resource records and resource detail views
- Search results and resource cards
- Phone numbers, directions, hours, eligibility, intake, and access instructions
- Emergency, shelter, crisis, and time-sensitive help pathways
- Call, directions, save, share, filter, and other help-navigation controls
- Any surface where an advertisement could be mistaken for a provider or official recommendation

No organization may pay for higher placement, preferred verification treatment, favorable wording, or exclusion of another legitimate resource.

## Permitted Advertising Surfaces

Tasteful, clearly labeled advertising may be considered only on non-urgent informational surfaces, including:

- The public area surrounding the globe, without obstructing its controls
- About RWN and organizational information pages
- Educational articles and general guides
- Outreach stories and public impact reports
- Volunteer, partnership, and other non-help-navigation pages

Advertisements must be manually placed, visually separated from RWN content, and labeled `Advertisement` or `Sponsored`. Pop-ups, autoplay media, interstitials, deceptive native ads, and ads positioned to cause accidental clicks are prohibited.

## Sponsorship Standard

Sponsor acknowledgments are preferred over intrusive advertising. Sponsorship must never influence:

- Which resources are included
- Search order or prominence
- Verification decisions
- Editorial language
- Corrections or removals
- Public impact reporting

Acceptable acknowledgment may state that a company or organization supports the RWN globe, a region, a category, outreach, or data maintenance. It must not imply that RWN endorses a sponsor’s products or services.

## Privacy and Safety

Advertising must not require RWN to expose a person’s searched need, selected resource, precise location, crisis status, or other sensitive behavior to advertisers. Any advertising technology must receive privacy, security, legal, nonprofit-accounting, and accessibility review before launch.

## Revenue Use

All advertising and sponsorship revenue must support RWN’s charitable mission, operations, data verification, outreach, staffing, technology, reserves, and other properly authorized organizational expenses. Revenue arrangements and related-party conflicts must be documented and reviewed under RWN governance and financial controls.

## Launch Rule

No advertisement or sponsorship placement may launch until RWN has a real organizational owner for the program, written approval standards, accounting treatment, privacy review, prohibited-advertiser rules, complaint handling, and a method to remove inappropriate ads promptly.

## Permanent Product Requirement

Every future RWN design, feature, partnership, and monetization decision must be tested against the core principle:

> **Are we supporting the platform, or are we monetizing someone while they are trying to get help?**

If the answer is uncertain, the help experience remains ad-free.
