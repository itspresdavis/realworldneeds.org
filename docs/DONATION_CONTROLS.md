# Donation Controls Before Launch

Donation processing must remain disabled until RWN can verify and operate all controls below.

## Organization And Policy

- Legal entity, jurisdiction, public address, responsible officers, and tax status are documented.
- Donation, refund, restricted-fund, privacy, safeguarding, sanctions, complaint, and correction policies are approved and published.
- Donor-facing language accurately describes tax treatment by jurisdiction and never assumes deductibility.

## Payment And Authorization

- The payment processor account belongs to the verified entity and settles to an approved bank account.
- Payment webhooks are signature-verified, idempotent, logged, and reconciled.
- Contribution records store processor references, currency, gross amount, fees, refunds, disputes, designation, and receipt status.
- Role separation covers campaign approval, fund allocation, payment release, reconciliation, and public reporting.

## Restricted Funds And Delivery

- Designations map to approved needs and cannot be silently reassigned.
- Overfunding, underfunding, expired needs, refunds, and residual balances have published treatment rules.
- Each disbursement links to authorization, recipient verification, allocation, and delivery evidence.
- Public reporting distinguishes committed, disbursed, delivered, refunded, and remaining balances.

## Reconciliation And Audit

- Processor settlements reconcile to the bank and internal ledger on a defined schedule.
- Chargebacks, disputes, refunds, corrections, and write-offs retain a visible history.
- Evidence and financial records follow documented retention, redaction, access, and deletion rules.
- Independent review or audit expectations are defined before scale or impact claims are published.
