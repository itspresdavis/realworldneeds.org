# Professional Launch Requirements

The public aid finder can operate as an informational directory. The following capabilities require additional verified organizational, legal, financial, security, and program controls.

## Public Identity

- Registered entity name, jurisdiction, registration number where public, and tax status
- Public postal address, monitored email, telephone, and complaint channel
- Board or governing body, executive accountability, conflicts policy, and annual reporting cadence
- Terms, privacy notice, accessibility statement, source correction policy, and safeguarding policy

## Need Intake And Case Operations

- Defined service scope and exclusion criteria
- Consent language, emergency escalation, identity minimization, and sensitive-location handling
- Partner due diligence, conflict checks, sanctions screening where applicable, and periodic reverification
- Case ownership, approval thresholds, evidence standards, expiry, closeout, appeal, and correction workflows

## Logistics

- Procurement authorization and vendor review
- Inventory custody, warehousing, shipment tracking, receipt, loss, damage, and exception handling
- Cash-transfer or service-delivery controls where physical logistics do not apply
- Country-specific compliance, insurance, export/import, and record retention where applicable

## Donations And Finance

- Verified banking and payment processor ownership
- Donation, refund, restricted-fund, anti-fraud, reconciliation, and reserve policies
- Double-entry accounting or an equivalent controlled ledger
- Segregated duties, approval limits, month-end close, financial statements, and external review expectations

## Technology And Data

- Authenticated role-based operator access with least privilege and multifactor authentication
- Separate private and public data, encryption, backups, recovery testing, audit logs, and incident response
- Data classification, retention, deletion, redaction, and cross-border transfer decisions
- Monitoring for availability, security events, failed payments, stale resource sources, and overdue case reviews

## Launch Rule

No workflow should be presented as live until its owner, policy, controls, escalation path, and public status are real and reviewable.
