# Real World Needs SEO

## Technical Foundation

The theme provides canonical URLs, unique resource titles, page-specific fallback descriptions, Open Graph metadata, Twitter metadata, responsive image markup, semantic headings, and Shopify sitemap compatibility.

Structured data is limited to information the store can verify:

- Organization data from public theme settings
- Article data from real Shopify articles
- Product and offer data from real Shopify products and variants
- Breadcrumbs for pages, blogs, articles, collections, and products
- FAQ data only on the visible FAQ page

The theme does not publish ratings, reviews, impact numbers, donation totals, partners, tax status, or legal claims unless real configured content supports them.

## Content Architecture

Use these primary search destinations:

- `/pages/give-clothing` for clothing donation guidance and future drive information
- `/pages/volunteer` for real volunteer openings
- `/pages/needs` for public needs and project discovery
- `/pages/transparency` for project records, spending, reports, and governance
- `/blogs/stories` for Good Around the World reporting and organization updates
- `/collections/supporter-shop` for mission merchandise

Each published page needs one clear H1, a specific Shopify title, a useful meta description, descriptive internal links, and factual copy that answers the visitor's question without keyword repetition.

## Stories

Stories are the main long-term organic growth channel. Publish work that is worth reading and properly sourced rather than filling a calendar.

Strong recurring formats include:

- A practical solution to a basic need
- A sourced Good Around the World report
- A Real World Needs project update
- A completed project record
- A community clothing guide
- A clear explainer about humanitarian logistics or transparency

Every external claim should link to a credible source. Use a real author, publication date, descriptive featured image, useful excerpt, and one primary category tag. Link each Story to relevant program, project, or transparency pages and link those pages back to useful Stories.

## Project Search Value

Every public project should have a descriptive title, location, category, short summary, verified need, proposed solution, responsible organization, status, last updated date, and final outcome when complete. Do not publish thin records just to create pages.

## Ongoing Review

Before publishing new content:

1. Confirm the page answers a real question.
2. Write a unique title and description.
3. Check heading order and image alt text.
4. Add specific internal links.
5. Cite external reporting.
6. Preview social sharing metadata.
7. Check the page on a phone.
8. Confirm no unsupported claim or hidden content was added.

Use Shopify Search Console and analytics only after privacy and cookie practices have been reviewed. Performance, indexing, and search queries should guide future improvements without changing the organization's claims to chase keywords.
