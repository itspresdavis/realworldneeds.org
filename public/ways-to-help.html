Ways to help

Start with what people actually need.

RWN is a free services directory today. We are also laying the groundwork for a transparent way to direct useful goods and financial support through trusted local partners.

RWN is not accepting money or goods at this time. There is no payment form, collection address or authorized fundraising campaign on this website. If anyone claims otherwise, do not send money or property.

1. Ways to help today

Support a listed organization directly

Use the directory to find a service provider, open its original-source link and follow the organization’s current giving instructions. Confirm that the website and payment page belong to that organization before contributing.

Share dependable information

Send someone a service listing, the text directory or the original provider page. A useful introduction can save time for a person who is already dealing with a difficult situation.

Check what is requested before donating goods

Food banks, shelters and community programs often have limited storage and specific health or safety rules. Ask the receiving organization what it can use, how items should be prepared and when donations can be accepted.

2. What RWN is building

The long-term aim is simple: publish a confirmed local need, match it with appropriate support and show what happened afterward. The operational details matter, so RWN will not describe this work as active until the responsible people, controls and partner agreements are in place.

  1. Confirm the need and receiving partner. A collection should begin with a specific request, a responsible organization and a practical delivery plan.
  2. Accept only appropriate support. Financial campaigns should explain permitted uses. Goods collections should name the items, condition, quantity and delivery requirements.
  3. Track each handoff. Purchasing, allocation, shipping and disbursement records should be reconciled without exposing the private details of people receiving help.
  4. Report the outcome. Supporters should be able to see what was received, what was delivered, what remains and how problems were handled.

3. What must be in place before giving opens

RWN will not accept a donation simply because a payment tool or collection address is available. The following safeguards are launch requirements:

  • a verified legal entity, responsible officers and published jurisdiction;
  • banking and payment accounts held by that entity, with access controls and reconciliation;
  • clear tax-status, receipt, fee, refund, restricted-fund and unused-balance language;
  • privacy and security terms covering donor, payment and delivery information;
  • written standards for acceptable goods, storage, transport, loss and disposal;
  • partner checks, safeguarding procedures and a monitored complaints channel; and
  • public reporting that can be checked against financial and delivery records.

The Transparency Center records what RWN has and has not published. That page should be checked before relying on any future fundraising claim.

4. What supporters should see when giving opens

A legitimate RWN collection will identify the responsible entity and receiving partner, describe the need in concrete terms, explain how money or goods may be used and state what happens if circumstances change. It will also link to the applicable privacy, refund, goods-acceptance and reporting terms.

RWN will not call a contribution tax-deductible unless the responsible entity and campaign qualify under the applicable law. It will not suggest that a donation gives someone faster access to services. Finding help through the directory will remain free.
