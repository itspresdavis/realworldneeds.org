A first drive in Raynham

Preston Davis talked through the idea with Pastor Donn Fisher. Real World Needs held its first clothing drive at Calvary Chapel Raynham, Massachusetts.

Michael Basiliere joined RWN as a partner in August 2026. The work brings together two ways of helping: getting essentials to people through local partners, and making it easier to find organizations already offering support.
