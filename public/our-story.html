Why we started.

People shouldn’t have to spend a morning calling around just to find a meal or a place to shower. That’s one reason we started Real World Needs.

A first drive in Raynham

Preston Davis talked over the idea with Pastor Donn Fisher, and we held our first clothing drive at Calvary Chapel Raynham. Michael Basiliere joined RWN as a partner in August 2026.

Two ways to help

We help get everyday supplies to people through local organizations. We also keep a directory for anyone who needs help when there isn’t a drive nearby.

We link to the organizations themselves so people can check hours and eligibility before making the trip. And when we share our own numbers, we’ll count what was handed out, not what’s still in storage.
