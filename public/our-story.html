Why RWN?

When someone needs a meal or a warm coat, it should be easy to find out where to go. We started Real World Needs to get everyday essentials to people and make help easier to find.

Help exists. Finding it is still hard.

A food pantry might be a few streets away. A clinic may offer free care. A local organization may have coats to give out. But if you do not know they exist, who they serve or how to reach them, that help can stay out of reach.

We started RWN because we wanted to do something useful about that gap. Alongside collecting and distributing essentials, we are bringing information about existing free resources into one place: food, water, clothing, shelter and healthcare.

Our aim is to build the largest useful directory of free essential resources. That means more than adding names. Each listing needs a source, clear access details and regular review. A small pantry with accurate hours is more useful than a long list of places you cannot actually use.

You should not have to explain yourself to search.

The directory is open to everyone. You can browse by place or need, save a useful listing on your device and contact the provider directly. Optional suggestions help narrow the list; they do not decide whether you qualify for assistance.

We want people to spend less time searching and more time getting what they need. That is the reason for the directory, and the standard we want to hold it to.

What we do now

We collect everyday supplies and work with local organizations to get them to people who need them. We also keep a directory of places where people can look for help.

The directory answers practical questions: where to go, when it’s open and who to call. We link to the provider so they can check those details. When we report what RWN distributed, we count what was handed out, not what’s still in storage.
