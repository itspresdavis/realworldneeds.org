Why we started

Free help already exists in food pantries, community clinics, shelters and neighborhood organizations. The information is scattered. Finding an address is one task; finding out who can go, what is free and when to arrive is another.

We started Real World Needs to make that search easier. We want someone looking for help to leave with a useful next step: a place to go, a number to call or an application to start.

A directory anyone can use

You can search RWN without paying or creating an account. Listings bring together the provider’s contact details, available services and any published access requirements. You can save useful listings on your device.

Some programs serve a particular area, age group or income level. We include those conditions where the source provides them, so people can check before making the trip.

The work ahead

Our ambition is to build the largest directory of free essential resources. There are still places and needs we do not cover well. Growing it means finding reliable local sources, checking access details and keeping up when services change.

If you run a service or know a resource we are missing, we would like to hear from you. A corrected address or a new listing can help the next person who searches.

Transparency

How we work, what our information means and what is still being built.

What RWN can help with

RWN brings together free essential services and official resource finders. Providers decide who can use their services. A listing cannot reserve a place, guarantee availability or provide emergency assistance.

Our coverage is incomplete. Some listings are places to visit; others are regional finders or networks. Several services can share one address. Directory totals are not a count of people helped.

How information is reviewed

We look for an original source, contact details, the area served, free-access conditions and a review date. A source check does not mean we called the provider or can confirm today’s hours and availability. Please check before you travel.

You can email a correction with the listing link and an official source. Changes are reviewed before publication; submitting a correction does not update the directory automatically.

Sources and open data

The reviewed directory is available as CSV and JSON, with source links and review dates. Optional live USDA meal-site results are not included in these downloads; they appear in the finder after a U.S. location search.

Keep the original source and review date if you reuse a listing. Release metadata identifies the published snapshot so it can be compared with the website.

Privacy and accessible browsing

No account is needed to search. Saved services stay on your device. The site does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics, and it does not collect personal case information. Exact location stays on your device; a U.S. meal-site search sends the USDA an approximate search area.

The text directory includes every reviewed catalog listing without the globe. Optional live USDA results appear in the finder after a location search. Supported browsers can reopen the saved directory after a connection drops. We are working toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA; we do not claim formal conformance.

Organization and donations

RWN has not yet published verified registration, board information or financial reports. It does not claim registered charity or nonprofit status. This website does not accept donations or personal requests for assistance.

Before online giving opens, we need verified legal and contact information, responsible leadership, safeguarding and complaints procedures, and financial controls. We will publish confirmed information here as it becomes available.
