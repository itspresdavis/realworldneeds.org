2. Emergency and urgent help

RWN is not an emergency service. It does not monitor requests, place emergency calls, dispatch responders, provide crisis counseling, or know whether a person reached a provider. Listings that mention emergency, crisis, health, domestic violence, trafficking, suicide, or legal services remain third-party resources; their own eligibility, safety, and response procedures apply.

Use your judgment about device privacy and personal safety. If opening a map, making a call, or visiting a site could put you at risk, use a safer device or contact a trusted person when possible.

3. What directory information means

RWN reviews listings against named public or provider sources and shows a review date. Even careful information can become outdated. Hours, address, eligibility, capacity, cost, languages, documentation requirements, geographic coverage, and program availability can change without notice.

  • Service site markers aim to show a public-facing physical service location.
  • Official locator markers represent the geographic scope or access point of a provider’s own locator, not every service site in that area.
  • Network markers represent a recognized network or program coverage area and may not be a walk-in location.

Check the linked original source and, when practical, contact the provider before travelling or relying on a listing. RWN does not decide eligibility, reserve capacity, make referrals, or guarantee an outcome.

4. Globe and map locations

The globe is a discovery interface, not a survey or navigation instrument. Direct-service coordinates are intended to identify the listed public location, but map imagery, national boundaries, labels, and coordinates can be generalized or imperfect. Locator and network coordinates are representative by design.

Apple Maps and Google Maps links open independent services. Review the destination, route, border, safety, and accessibility information before travel.
