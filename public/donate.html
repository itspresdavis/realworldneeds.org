Donations

We are not accepting donations yet.

Real World Needs does not currently accept money, goods, or pledges through this website or any other channel.

Last updated October 20, 2026

Status: Real World Needs has not yet published a legal entity, tax identification number, or tax-exempt status. Until that information is public, we do not ask for or accept donations, and no gift to RWN is tax-deductible.

Why not yet

We want anyone who supports RWN to be able to see who runs it and how money is handled before any is accepted. When that changes, this page and the Transparency Center will be updated first.

Be cautious of requests in our name

If anyone asks you to donate to “Real World Needs” today, the request did not come from this website. Please do not send money.

Ways to help now

If you want to support people directly, the services listed in the directory are run by their own organizations. Contact them through the links on each listing to learn whether they accept support.

If you need help, start at Find help.
