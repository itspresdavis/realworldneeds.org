Privacy

We collect as little as possible.

You can search without an account. RWN does not ask for your name, the help you need, your immigration status, medical information, or case details.

In brief: RWN has no accounts, advertising trackers, marketing cookies, or third-party analytics service, and we do not sell personal information. Saved services stay in your browser. If you choose location, your exact coordinates stay on your device. Supported browsers can keep public directory files for low-connectivity use; search words are not stored in that offline cache. A small amount of first-party technical diagnostic data is deliberately reduced to broad page and screen-size classes before it is sent.

1. Who and what this covers

Real World Needs (“RWN”) operates the public directory at realworldneeds.org. This notice covers the website and its browser-based tools. It does not cover the independent organizations, map services, hotlines, or government sites linked from a listing.

RWN currently provides an informational directory only. The site does not offer accounts, application forms, donations, case intake, or direct messaging.

2. Information the site uses

The site uses only the information needed for the features you choose and for basic reliability:

  • search words and filters, processed in your browser and not included in RWN diagnostic requests;
  • the identifiers of services you save, stored in your browser;
  • your device location, only with your permission, used temporarily to sort services by distance;
  • a location rounded to one decimal degree when your browser requests current nearby USDA summer meal sites;
  • a service identifier in the part of a shared link after #;
  • public directory files held in browser Cache Storage for low-connectivity use on supported browsers;
  • coarse first-party performance and error diagnostics described below; and
  • standard network and security information processed by our hosting provider when your browser requests a page.

RWN does not use this website to build a profile of your needs or make decisions about your eligibility.

3. How location works

The site asks for location only after you press Use my location and your browser requests permission. Your exact coordinates stay in browser memory while the page is open so services can be sorted by distance. They are not saved, added to a shared link, or sent to an RWN database.

In the United States, your browser rounds latitude and longitude to one decimal degree before asking the public USDA Food and Nutrition Service ArcGIS map for current sites within 160 kilometres. This gives the USDA and Esri an approximate area—roughly 11 kilometres of latitude—and the network address used by your browser, but not your exact coordinates. The request happens only after you approve location. See Esri’s privacy statement and the USDA privacy policy.

You can stop location sorting and remove loaded meal sites with the Clear button beside the location control, Clear browser data in the footer, by closing the page, or by changing your browser’s location permission.

If you open Apple Maps or Google Maps, that service receives the organization’s name or public map coordinates. RWN does not put your device location in the link. Your map provider may use location separately under its own settings and privacy notice.

4. Cookies, saved services, and links

RWN does not set application cookies. When you press Save, the site uses local storage under the key rwn-saved-resources to keep a list of service identifiers on that device. The list remains until you remove saved services, clear RWN browser data, or clear site data in your browser.

Shared service links use the part of the URL after #. Browsers do not include that fragment in the HTTP request sent to the hosting server. Older RWN links that used a query parameter are read and immediately converted to the fragment format in the browser.

Technical performance sampling is chosen randomly when a page loads and does not create a cookie, local-storage value, or persistent telemetry identifier.

5. Low-connectivity and offline directory files

On browsers that support service workers, RWN can keep a small set of public same-site files in browser Cache Storage so the lightweight directory can reopen after a connection drops. The cached set is limited to the directory shell, its styles and script, the public RWN mark and web-app manifest, and the static directory search index. Directory pages you visit can also be kept so they can reopen without a network connection.

The offline worker does not cache RWN API requests, does not store the words you type into search, does not create a search-history database, and does not intentionally cache individual service-detail pages. Cache keys discard URL query strings. When a requested page is unavailable offline, navigation falls back to the cached text directory rather than inventing or serving stale live-source information.

These public files stay on your device. A newer RWN release can replace the cache, your browser can evict it, and you can remove it with Clear browser data in the finder footer or by clearing site data in browser settings.

6. Privacy-minimized technical diagnostics

RWN uses a small first-party endpoint on the same website to understand whether pages load and behave reliably. Performance timing is sampled on about 2% of page loads. Client errors and WebGL failures can also generate a limited diagnostic event when they occur.

Before a diagnostic request is sent, the browser reduces the page to a broad class such as finder, service, directory, or policy, and reduces screen width to small, medium, or large. Performance events can include measurements such as largest contentful paint, interaction latency, first contentful paint, time to first byte, layout shift, DOM readiness, and long-task count. Error events can include a short error type, script filename, and line or column number.

The diagnostic payload does not include your search words, exact page path, service identifier, city, precise screen dimensions, device location, saved services, reduced-motion preference, network connection type, name, email address, or an RWN user identifier. The endpoint does not return a tracking cookie.

7. Hosting and security logs

RWN is hosted by Vercel. Like most hosting services, Vercel processes request and device data needed to deliver and protect the site, which can include an IP address, browser or device information, requested path, time, and security events. Vercel may derive a general city or country from an IP address.

RWN has not added a third-party analytics or advertising tool. The privacy-minimized technical diagnostics described above are sent to an RWN endpoint hosted on Vercel, so Vercel necessarily processes the network request used to deliver that event. Where a legal basis is required, this processing supports delivery of the service you requested and RWN’s legitimate interests in site security, reliability, and understanding technical failures. Hosting data is retained under the applicable Vercel service configuration and legal requirements. See Vercel’s privacy notice.

8. When information is shared

RWN does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Information can be processed by:

  • Vercel, to host, deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the website and process the minimized technical diagnostic endpoint;
  • your browser and device provider, when you use location, local storage, or offline Cache Storage;
  • USDA’s public ArcGIS data service, which receives a rounded search point only after a U.S. location search; and
  • third-party service or map sites, only when you choose to open their links.

Vercel and linked providers may process data in countries other than your own. Their own contractual safeguards and privacy notices govern that processing.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

You can use the directory without sharing your location. You can block or revoke location permission, remove saved services, clear RWN’s offline cache and other site data, and open organization links without saving anything in RWN.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Most information used by the RWN interface is already under your control on your device. Use Clear browser data in the finder footer to remove RWN local storage, current location sorting, and the RWN offline cache. You can also clear site data in your browser. For information controlled by Vercel, see Vercel’s privacy request information.

Contact: RWN does not yet have a verified public privacy contact and does not accept personal case, account, donor, or contact information through this site. A privacy contact will be published before any of those features are introduced.

10. Children

The directory can be used to find public services, but it is not designed to collect information from children. RWN does not knowingly ask a child to submit personal information through this site. A child facing danger should contact a trusted adult or the appropriate local emergency service.

11. Changes to this notice

RWN will update the effective date and this page when its privacy practices materially change. If a future feature adds accounts, forms, third-party analytics, donations, or communications, the notice and consent controls must be updated before that feature is launched.
