Privacy

We collect as little as possible.

You can search without an account. RWN does not ask for your name, the help you need, your immigration status, medical information, or case details.

In brief: RWN has no accounts, advertising trackers, or analytics service, and we do not sell personal information. Saved services stay in your browser. If you choose location, your exact coordinates stay on your device. For U.S. summer meal results, the USDA receives only an approximate search area.

1. Who and what this covers

Real World Needs (“RWN”) operates the public directory at realworldneeds.org. This notice covers the website and its browser-based tools. It does not cover the independent organizations, map services, hotlines, or government sites linked from a listing.

RWN currently provides an informational directory only. The site does not offer accounts, application forms, donations, case intake, or direct messaging.

2. Information the site uses

The site uses only the information needed for the features you choose:

  • search words and filters, processed in your browser;
  • the identifiers of services you save, stored in your browser;
  • your device location, only with your permission, used temporarily to sort services by distance;
  • a location rounded to one decimal degree when your browser requests current nearby USDA summer meal sites;
  • a service identifier in the part of a shared link after #; and
  • standard network and security information processed by our hosting provider when your browser requests a page.

RWN does not use this website to build a profile of your needs or make decisions about your eligibility.

3. How location works

The site asks for location only after you press Use my location and your browser requests permission. Your exact coordinates stay in browser memory while the page is open so services can be sorted by distance. They are not saved, added to a shared link, or sent to an RWN database.

In the United States, your browser rounds latitude and longitude to one decimal degree before asking the public USDA Food and Nutrition Service ArcGIS map for current sites within 160 kilometres. This gives the USDA and Esri an approximate area—roughly 11 kilometres of latitude—and the network address used by your browser, but not your exact coordinates. The request happens only after you approve location. See Esri’s privacy statement and the USDA privacy policy.

You can stop location sorting and remove loaded meal sites with the Clear button beside the location control, Clear browser data in the footer, by closing the page, or by changing your browser’s location permission.

If you open Apple Maps or Google Maps, that service receives the organization’s name or public map coordinates. RWN does not put your device location in the link. Your map provider may use location separately under its own settings and privacy notice.

4. Cookies, saved services, and links

RWN does not set application cookies. When you press Save, the site uses local storage under the key rwn-saved-resources to keep a list of service identifiers on that device. The list remains until you remove saved services, clear RWN browser data, or clear site data in your browser.

Shared service links use the part of the URL after #. Browsers do not include that fragment in the HTTP request sent to the hosting server. Older RWN links that used a query parameter are read and immediately converted to the fragment format in the browser.

5. Hosting and security logs

RWN is hosted by Vercel. Like most hosting services, Vercel processes request and device data needed to deliver and protect the site, which can include an IP address, browser or device information, requested path, time, and security events. Vercel may derive a general city or country from an IP address. RWN has not added a third-party analytics or advertising tool.

Where a legal basis is required, this processing supports delivery of the service you requested and RWN’s legitimate interests in site security, reliability, and understanding technical failures. Hosting data is retained under the applicable Vercel service configuration and legal requirements. See Vercel’s privacy notice.

6. When information is shared

RWN does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Information can be processed by:

  • Vercel, to host, deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the website;
  • your browser and device provider, when you use location or local storage;
  • USDA’s public ArcGIS data service, which receives a rounded search point only after a U.S. location search; and
  • third-party service or map sites, only when you choose to open their links.

Vercel and linked providers may process data in countries other than your own. Their own contractual safeguards and privacy notices govern that processing.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You can use the directory without sharing your location. You can block or revoke location permission, remove saved services, clear site data, and open organization links without saving anything in RWN.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Most information used by the RWN interface is already under your control on your device. Use Clear browser data in the footer to remove RWN local storage and current location sorting. For information controlled by Vercel, see Vercel’s privacy request information.

Contact: RWN does not yet have a verified public privacy contact and does not accept personal case, account, donor, or contact information through this site. A privacy contact will be published before any of those features are introduced.

8. Children

The directory can be used to find public services, but it is not designed to collect information from children. RWN does not knowingly ask a child to submit personal information through this site. A child facing danger should contact a trusted adult or the appropriate local emergency service.

9. Changes to this notice

RWN will update the effective date and this page when its privacy practices materially change. If a future feature adds accounts, forms, analytics, donations, or communications, the notice and consent controls must be updated before that feature is launched.
