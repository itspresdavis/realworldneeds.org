Privacy

We collect as little as possible.

You can search without an account. This notice distinguishes information RWN actually collects from information that stays on your device or goes directly to a service you choose.

In brief: RWN collects a small amount of coarse first-party technical diagnostic data and our hosting provider necessarily processes ordinary request/security information. Search words, exact location, and saved services are not sent to an RWN analytics database. RWN offers optional contributor accounts and has no advertising trackers, marketing cookies, user-upload storage, payments, subscriptions, or user-facing AI assistant, and we do not sell personal information.

1. Who and what this covers

Real World Needs (“RWN”) operates the public directory at realworldneeds.org. This notice covers the website and its browser-based tools. It does not cover the independent organizations, map services, hotlines, government sites, or other third-party destinations linked from a listing.

RWN currently provides an informational directory only. The public directory needs no account. The contributor workspace offers accounts for catalog review. RWN does not offer service application forms, donations, case intake, direct messaging, purchases, subscriptions, free trials, recurring billing, file uploads, or a conversational AI service.

2. Information RWN collects or receives

RWN collects or receives only limited information needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the website:

  • Coarse technical diagnostics: broad page class, broad screen-size class, limited performance measurements, and limited client-error details sent to RWN’s same-site diagnostic endpoint as described below.
  • Ordinary hosting and security information: when your browser requests the site, Vercel necessarily processes network and request information such as IP address, browser/device information, requested path, time, and security events in order to deliver and protect the service.

RWN does not use this information to build a profile of the help you seek, determine eligibility, target advertising, or rank people.

Optional personal accounts

When personal accounts are available, you may choose to store broad resource topics and an optional city, city and country, or U.S. ZIP in your account. Saving requires your agreement each time. Health and shelter topics are stored only when you select them and agree. We do not ask for diagnoses, an exact address, income, documents or a personal story. Guest suggestions stay in your browser and are never automatically copied into your account.

Your account answers are private to your signed-in account through database access rules. Authorized infrastructure administrators can maintain the service. Answers are used to match published directory listings, not to determine eligibility, provide medical advice or sell advertising. They are not sent to AI services or included in diagnostics. Suggestions explain the matching topics; provider availability must still be checked.

You can download or immediately delete saved answers from Your resources. They remain saved until you replace or delete them. Account deletion requests are processed by the administrator; the account stays active until processed, and deleting the account removes its preference record. Provider backups may retain deleted data according to their backup lifecycle. Saved listings on your device remain separate. On a shared device, sign out when finished.

3. Information RWN does not collect from the finder

The following information is used locally or sent only when you choose a feature, but is not collected into an RWN user database:

  • Search words and filters are processed in your browser and are excluded from RWN diagnostic payloads.
  • Saved service identifiers stay in your browser’s local storage.
  • Directory suggestions: the topics you select and the optional city or ZIP are used only in your browser. Location, health and shelter choices are never remembered. If you opt in, only the Food, Water, Hygiene and Clothing topic labels are remembered on this browser. Anyone using it can see them. Use Reset suggestions to remove those labels, or clear site data in your browser.
  • Interface language: your English or Spanish preference stays in this browser and is not sent in diagnostic events. Clear browser data removes this preference.
  • Exact device location is used in browser memory only after you grant permission and is not sent to an RWN database.
  • Shared service links: Finder links for reviewed catalog listings place the service identifier after # in the URL, which browsers do not send in the page request. Links shared from a service detail page use that page’s ordinary /service/ address. Opening a service-page link sends that public path to the hosting provider.
  • Public offline directory files are stored in your browser’s Cache Storage on supported devices.

RWN does not ask you to upload documents or photos, and there is no user-upload storage bucket. Public static website storage contains only intentionally public files such as site code, styles, logos, map/data assets, and directory files.

The public website does not provide an AI chatbot or personalized generative-AI answer system. RWN does not send your search words, location, saved services, or RWN diagnostic payloads to an AI model.

4. How location works

The site asks for location only after you press Use my location and your browser requests permission. Your exact coordinates stay in browser memory while the page is open so services can be sorted by distance. They are not saved, added to a shared link, or sent to an RWN database.

In the United States, your browser rounds latitude and longitude to one decimal degree before asking the public USDA Food and Nutrition Service ArcGIS map for current sites within 160 kilometres. This gives the USDA and Esri an approximate area—roughly 11 kilometres of latitude—and the network address used by your browser, but not your exact coordinates. The request happens only after you approve location. See Esri’s privacy statement and the USDA privacy policy.

You can stop location sorting and remove loaded meal sites with the Clear button beside the location control, Clear browser data in the footer, by closing the page, or by changing your browser’s location permission.

If you open Apple Maps or Google Maps, that service receives the organization’s name or public map coordinates. RWN does not put your device location in the link. Your map provider may use location separately under its own settings and privacy notice.

Contributor accounts

The optional contributor workspace stores your email, authentication records, review notes, and any account-deletion request in Supabase. Authorized editors can see review notes and catalog change history. Public directory visitors cannot read contributor records. Editorial history includes the editor’s account identifier and before-and-after listing facts.

Account sessions use essential HTTP-only cookies. Account pages, account API responses, and private notes are excluded from the offline cache. Workspace searches are sent to RWN and Supabase to retrieve catalog records; public directory searches still run in your browser.

You can download your contributor information or request account deletion in workspace settings. An administrator must process deletion requests. Account and note records remain until deletion; editorial evidence remains with the public catalog’s history. No automated deletion deadline is promised. Please do not put personal case information into a review note.

Correction reporting

Public correction reports are not currently accepted. Before opening this channel, RWN will publish the information collected, who can review it, its retention period, and the deletion process. Do not send medical details, identification, payment information or personal case information through a listing correction.

Check correction reporting status. The finder remains available without an account or a report.

5. Cookies, saved services, and links

Public directory browsing does not require application cookies. Contributor sign-in uses essential, secure, HTTP-only session cookies. When you press Save, the site uses local storage under the key rwn-saved-resources to keep a list of service identifiers on that device. The list stays in that browser and does not sync between devices or accounts. Anyone using the same browser can see it. Open Saved services to remove a listing or clear the whole list. It remains until you remove saved services, clear RWN browser data, or clear site data in your browser.

Finder links for reviewed catalog listings place the service identifier after # in the URL, which browsers do not send in the page request. Links shared from a service detail page use that page’s ordinary /service/ address. Opening a service-page link sends that public path to the hosting provider. Sharing live USDA results creates a Google Maps link. Older finder links with a resource query parameter are converted to the fragment format after the page loads.

Technical performance sampling is chosen randomly when a page loads and does not create a cookie, local-storage value, or persistent telemetry identifier.

6. Low-connectivity and offline directory files

On browsers that support service workers, RWN can keep a small set of public same-site files in browser Cache Storage so the lightweight directory can reopen after a connection drops. The cached set is limited to the directory shell, its styles and script, the official RWN logo and web-app manifest, and the static directory search index. Directory pages you visit can also be kept so they can reopen without a network connection.

The offline worker does not cache RWN API requests, does not store the words you type into search, does not create a search-history database, and does not intentionally cache individual service-detail pages. Cache keys discard URL query strings. When a requested page is unavailable offline, navigation falls back to the cached text directory rather than inventing or serving stale live-source information.

These public files stay on your device. A bounded set of version-matched scripts and styles is retained across updates so already-open pages can keep working; it contains no directory records or saved-list contents. A newer RWN release can replace the cache, your browser can evict it, and you can remove it with Clear browser data in the finder footer or by clearing site data in browser settings.

Correction reports

Public correction reporting is currently disabled. When enabled, reports may include listing or service identity, issue details, a proposed correction, a source URL and an optional reply email. Reports stay private for authorized review and do not automatically change listings. Do not include health, identification, payment or hardship details. A monitored owner and retention period must be approved and published before reporting opens.

7. Privacy-minimized technical diagnostics

RWN uses a small first-party endpoint on the same website to understand whether pages load and behave reliably. Performance timing is sampled on about 2% of page loads. Client errors and WebGL failures can also generate a limited diagnostic event when they occur.

Before a diagnostic request is sent, the browser reduces the page to a broad class such as finder, service, directory, or policy, and reduces screen width to small, medium, or large. Performance events can include largest-contentful-paint observations, first paint, time to first byte, DOM readiness, long-task counts, the largest observed event duration and total observed layout shifts. The latter two are diagnostic values, not standards-compliant INP or CLS. Unsupported values remain unmeasured. Background starts and browser back/forward-cache restorations are excluded from this limited navigation sample. Error events include only an allowlisted error category; exception text, filenames and source URLs are excluded. Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track disables these client diagnostics.

The diagnostic payload does not include your search words, exact page path, service identifier, city, precise screen dimensions, device location, saved services, reduced-motion preference, network connection type, name, email address, or an RWN user identifier. The endpoint does not return a tracking cookie.

9. Retention and deletion

RWN follows a data-minimization approach rather than keeping a user profile:

  • Exact location: kept only in browser memory for the active page session or until you clear location.
  • Saved services: remain in your browser until you remove them or clear site data.
  • Offline directory files: remain in browser Cache Storage until a new release replaces them, the browser evicts them, or you clear RWN/browser site data.
  • Search words and filters: are processed locally and excluded from RWN diagnostic payloads. RWN keeps search and filter state in your browser’s page history so Back and Forward can restore your place. This is not an RWN analytics record.
  • RWN diagnostic events: are written to Vercel runtime logs; RWN does not copy them into a separate visitor analytics database or attach a persistent RWN user identifier.
  • Hosting/security logs: are retained under the active Vercel service configuration and applicable legal/security requirements rather than a separate RWN user-record system.

Use Clear browser data in the finder footer or your browser’s site-data controls to delete RWN local storage and offline cache. Because RWN intentionally does not attach an account or persistent visitor ID to runtime and hosting logs, RWN may not be able to reliably isolate a particular low-information server log entry as belonging to one visitor.

10. Security controls

RWN uses HTTPS and restrictive browser/security headers, limits browser capabilities the site does not need, blocks third-party script execution by default, keeps the diagnostic endpoint same-origin, and minimizes the information sent to that endpoint. The public site does not expose a user-upload bucket, payment form or unprotected administrative interface.

No security control can guarantee absolute protection. RWN therefore reduces risk primarily by not collecting sensitive visitor information that the directory does not need.

11. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track

RWN does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or run cross-site advertising trackers. Because those activities are not present, Global Privacy Control (GPC) and browser “Do Not Track” signals do not need to disable a separate sale, sharing, or advertising-tracking feature on this site. Your browser’s normal blocking, location, storage, and permission controls continue to work.

If RWN later introduces a practice for which an opt-out preference signal is legally required, the signal handling and this notice must be updated before that practice launches.

12. Your choices and privacy rights

You can use the directory without sharing your location. You can block or revoke location permission, remove saved services, clear RWN’s offline cache and other site data, and open organization links without saving anything in RWN.

Depending on where you live and whether a privacy law applies to RWN, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Most information used by the RWN interface is already under your control on your device. Use Clear browser data in the finder footer to remove RWN local storage, current location sorting, and the RWN offline cache. You can also clear site data in your browser. For information controlled by Vercel, see Vercel’s privacy request information.

Privacy contact: RWN does not yet have a verified public privacy mailbox and does not accept personal case or donor information through this site. Contributors can export their own notes and request account deletion in workspace settings. Workspace requests are stored for the project administrator to review; they are not an emergency or case-support channel.

13. Children

The directory can be used to find public services, but it is not designed to collect information from children. RWN does not knowingly ask a child to submit personal information through this site. A child facing danger should contact a trusted adult or the appropriate local emergency service.

14. Changes to this notice

RWN will update the review date and this page when its privacy practices materially change. Before introducing new data uses, user uploads, third-party analytics, advertising, payments, subscriptions, donations, direct communications, or user-facing generative AI, RWN must review the data flow, security controls, consent/choice requirements, retention/deletion process, and this privacy notice.
